Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

LongClarity is a household retirement-planning and educational modeling application. This Privacy Policy explains how LongClarity handles information when you use the app.

LongClarity is designed so that the personal and financial information used to build your retirement plan is primarily processed and stored locally on your device. LongClarity does not operate a backend server that receives or stores your retirement plan.

1. Information Used by LongClarity

To build and maintain your retirement model, LongClarity may use information you enter into the app, including:

  • names of household members;
  • dates of birth and ages;
  • retirement timing;
  • household income;
  • estimated monthly spending;
  • retirement account and other asset balances;
  • savings information;
  • Social Security assumptions;
  • pension assumptions;
  • healthcare assumptions;
  • planned future spending changes;
  • retirement-plan assumptions and scenarios;
  • changes you choose to adopt as your Current Plan; and
  • information you update through LongClarity Coach Check-Ins.

This information may include personal and financial information. It is used to perform retirement modeling and to keep your plan available between app sessions.

2. Where Your Retirement-Plan Information Is Stored

LongClarity stores substantive retirement-plan information locally on your device using protected application storage.

LongClarity does not operate a cloud-storage or synchronization service that receives or stores your retirement-plan information on LongClarity servers.

Your locally stored application data may also be included in device backups performed through Apple's iOS or iCloud backup services. Apple's device-backup services are controlled by Apple and are separate from LongClarity's own storage and backup features.

3. How LongClarity Uses Your Information to Provide the App

Information you enter into your retirement plan is used to provide LongClarity's features, including to:

  • build and update your retirement model;
  • calculate modeled retirement outcomes;
  • compare your Current Plan with proposed scenarios;
  • allow you to explore changes without automatically changing your Current Plan;
  • provide educational modeling through LongClarity Coach;
  • perform Coach Check-Ins;
  • model Planned Spending Changes;
  • generate retirement charts and year-by-year information; and
  • create reports and other exports you request.

These retirement-model calculations are performed locally on your device. LongClarity does not send the underlying retirement-plan information to a LongClarity server for these calculations.

4. Product Analytics

LongClarity uses PostHog for limited product-usage analytics. These analytics help us understand how LongClarity is being used and improve the app.

What LongClarity sends to PostHog

LongClarity sends limited product-usage events, such as whether a feature was opened, a retirement plan was built or adopted, a Coach feature was used, or a subscription-related action occurred.

These events may include general app information such as the app version, app build, build type, and categorical information about the feature, Coach Play, or step being used.

What LongClarity does not send to PostHog

LongClarity does not include the underlying personal or financial values from your retirement plan in the analytics events it sends to PostHog.

For example, the dollar amounts you enter for income, spending, savings, retirement assets, pensions, or healthcare are not included as properties of these analytics events.

PostHog may separately process technical, session, device, or network information as part of providing its analytics service.

LongClarity has disabled PostHog session replay, automatic screen views, automatic element interactions, and automatic lifecycle events.

LongClarity does not use PostHog to create a LongClarity customer profile tied to your name or email address.

LongClarity does not use PostHog analytics for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Purchases and Subscriptions

LongClarity uses Apple's App Store and StoreKit for Premium purchases and subscription management.

What Apple handles

Purchases and subscriptions are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple handles the transaction through your Apple account and Apple's payment systems.

What LongClarity receives

LongClarity receives the StoreKit information needed to determine whether Premium access is available, including relevant purchase, renewal, expiration, and restore status.

What LongClarity does not receive

LongClarity does not operate its own payment processor and does not receive your full payment-card information through the app.

Apple's handling of information associated with your Apple account, App Store purchases, and subscriptions is governed by Apple's own privacy practices and terms.

6. LongClarity Backup Files

LongClarity allows you to create a .longclaritybackup file so that relevant LongClarity plan and application information can later be restored.

What a LongClarity backup contains

When you choose to create a LongClarity backup, the backup may contain substantial personal and financial information from your retirement plan so that relevant plan and app information can later be restored.

How the backup is protected

LongClarity backup files are not password-encrypted by LongClarity.

You should treat a .longclaritybackup file as sensitive and store or share it accordingly.

What happens when you export it

You choose where the backup is saved or shared.

Once a backup is exported outside LongClarity, that copy is under your control and may also be handled by the application, storage provider, device, or person you selected.

LongClarity cannot recall or delete copies you have exported elsewhere.

7. Retirement Reports, PDFs, and Sharing

What the report may contain

Reports and PDFs you generate can contain substantial personal and financial information from your retirement plan.

Where the report is created

LongClarity generates these documents on your device. LongClarity does not upload the contents of the report or PDF to a LongClarity server.

Product analytics

LongClarity may record that a Professional Retirement Plan was generated for product-usage analytics, but the report itself and the underlying financial values contained in it are not included in that analytics event.

What happens when you share or save it

If you choose to save or share a report, LongClarity uses Apple's sharing functionality. You choose the destination.

The application, storage service, device, or person you select may then receive and store the document.

After it leaves LongClarity

Once you export a report or PDF, LongClarity cannot control, recall, or delete copies stored or shared outside the app.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

How long your plan stays on your device

Your retirement-plan information is stored locally so that your Current Plan and related information remain available when you return to LongClarity.

LongClarity does not automatically delete your active retirement plan after a set period.

Removing LongClarity data from your device

LongClarity does not operate a retirement-plan backend or consumer account system through which your locally stored plan can be remotely deleted.

Deleting LongClarity from your device is the primary way to remove the app's live local application data from that installation, subject to Apple's separate device and iCloud backup behavior.

What deleting the app does not remove

Deleting LongClarity does not necessarily remove information contained in previous Apple device or iCloud backups, backup files or PDFs you previously exported, or copies you shared or saved elsewhere.

Deleting LongClarity also does not cancel an Apple App Store subscription or delete Apple's transaction or account records.

Previously transmitted analytics

Limited product-usage analytics that were previously transmitted to PostHog are separate from the retirement-plan information stored locally on your device.

Deleting LongClarity from your device does not itself selectively erase analytics information previously transmitted to PostHog.

9. Apple Device and iCloud Backups

LongClarity does not operate a cloud-storage or synchronization service for your retirement-plan information. Your plan is stored locally on your device.

Apple backups are separate

Information stored by LongClarity on your device may also be included in device backups performed through Apple's iOS or iCloud backup services.

Those backup services are provided and controlled by Apple, not LongClarity.

LongClarity backups are different

Apple's device and iCloud backup system is separate from the .longclaritybackup feature in LongClarity.

A .longclaritybackup file is created only when you choose to use LongClarity's backup feature.

Apple's handling of iCloud, device backups, and related information is governed by Apple's own privacy practices and terms.

10. Advertising and Tracking

LongClarity does not display third-party advertising and does not use your LongClarity activity to serve you targeted advertising.

LongClarity does not use Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) or track your activity across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

LongClarity does use PostHog for the limited product analytics described above. Those analytics help us understand how LongClarity is being used; they are not used by LongClarity for advertising.

11. Children and Younger Users

LongClarity does not impose an 18+ restriction.

LongClarity is a retirement-planning and educational modeling application intended primarily for people and households interested in understanding retirement planning. Its educational modeling tools may also be used by younger people to learn about long-term financial concepts.

LongClarity does not require consumers to create an account and does not operate an age-verification system.

LongClarity's planned App Store age-rating direction is 4+, subject to the rating produced by Apple's applicable age-rating questionnaire.

12. Security

Protecting information on your device

LongClarity stores substantive retirement-plan information using protected local application storage provided through iOS.

LongClarity does not operate a server that receives or stores your retirement plan.

Protecting information you export

Backup files, retirement reports, and other documents you choose to export can contain substantial personal and financial information.

The security of an exported copy also depends on where you choose to save or share it.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We encourage you to protect access to your device and use appropriate care when storing or sharing exported LongClarity information.

13. Third-Party Services

LongClarity relies on a limited number of third-party services.

PostHog — Product Analytics

LongClarity uses PostHog for the limited product-usage analytics described in this Policy.

The underlying personal and financial values in your retirement plan are not included as properties of the analytics events LongClarity sends to PostHog.

PostHog may separately process technical, session, device, or network information in providing its service.

Apple — App Store, StoreKit, and Device Services

Apple provides services used for LongClarity purchases and subscriptions and may provide device services such as iOS or iCloud backup.

These Apple services are separate from LongClarity's local retirement-plan storage.

Services or people you choose when exporting information

When you deliberately save or share a backup, report, PDF, or other document, the destination you select may receive that information.

That destination may process or store the information under its own privacy practices.

LongClarity does not operate a backend that receives or stores your substantive retirement-plan information.

14. Your Choices

LongClarity is designed to leave important decisions about your information and retirement plan with you.

Information you enter

You choose the information and assumptions you enter into your retirement model.

Changes to your Current Plan

Exploring a scenario does not automatically change your Current Plan.

Your Current Plan changes only when you deliberately review and adopt proposed changes.

Backups and exports

You choose whether to create a LongClarity backup or save or share a retirement report, PDF, or other export.

You also choose where that information is sent or stored.

Removing local information

If you no longer want your active LongClarity retirement-plan information stored on a particular device, you may delete LongClarity from that device, subject to the limitations concerning Apple backups, exported information, and previously transmitted analytics described elsewhere in this Policy.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when LongClarity's features, data practices, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the Last Updated date shown at the top of this Policy.

If a change materially affects how LongClarity handles your information, we may also provide additional notice within the app or through another appropriate method.
